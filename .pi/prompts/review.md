Perform a DESIGN REVIEW on the current or most recent block.

## Review Criteria

### Originality
- Does this feel handcrafted or AI-generated?
- Would it stand out on Awwwards?
- Is there anything surprising or memorable?

### Visual Hierarchy
- Is the focal point clear?
- Does scale/spacing/contrast guide the eye intentionally?
- Are there at least 3 levels of visual depth?

### Emotional Impact
- What emotion does this evoke?
- Is the emotional tone consistent with DESIGN_DIRECTION.md?

### Premium Feel
- Typography: large enough? intentional weight? proper rhythm?
- Spacing: intentional or formulaic? enough breathing room?
- Motion: purposeful or decorative?

### AI Detection
Check for: generic layouts, repeated sections, startup hero patterns, dashboard aesthetics, equal card grids, AI purple gradients, fade-up everything.

### Mobile
- Is mobile a dedicated composition (not just scaled desktop)?
- Does the emotional impact survive on small screens?

## Output Format

List each issue with severity (🔴 Critical / 🟡 Warning / 🔵 Improvement).
For each: what's wrong, why it matters, and a specific fix.
