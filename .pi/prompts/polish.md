Perform a POLISH PASS on the current or most recent block.

## Polish Checklist

### Transitions
- Between-section transitions: smooth? intentional?
- In-section reveals: do they feel physical and premium?
- Scroll-driven animations: properly timed?

### Hover States
- All interactive elements have hover states?
- Hover states feel premium (not just opacity change)?
- Transitions have appropriate easing (not linear)?

### Typography
- Line heights: consistent rhythm?
- Letter spacing: intentional?
- Font weights: proper hierarchy?
- Responsive scaling: readable at all breakpoints?

### Spacing
- Vertical rhythm consistent?
- Padding/margins use design tokens (not arbitrary px)?
- Whitespace is intentional, not leftover?

### Microinteractions
- Buttons have press states?
- Links have transition?
- Loading states if applicable?

### Visual Tension
- Are there moments of contrast (light/dark, big/small, dense/airy)?
- Is there breathing room after intense sections?
- Does the rhythm vary intentionally?

## Output

List improvements with specific CSS/code changes. Focus on high-impact, low-effort wins first.
