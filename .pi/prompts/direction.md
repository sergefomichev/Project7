Generate 3 creative directions for $1.

For each direction, define:

### Direction N: [Name]

**Visual Identity**
- Composition approach
- Color/contrast strategy
- Typography treatment
- Depth/layering

**Motion Concept**
- How things enter
- How the user moves through
- Key animation moments

**Interaction Model**
- How the user engages
- What triggers motion
- Unique interaction idea

**Emotional Impact**
- What the user feels
- What they remember

**Risks**
- What could go wrong
- Complexity concerns

**Reference Extraction**
- What patterns from references inspired this
- What was reinterpreted

## After presenting all 3

Recommend one direction and explain why it best fits the project's design philosophy (premium, editorial, memorable, non-generic).
